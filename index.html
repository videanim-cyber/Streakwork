          How scoring works
          • Finishing a habit earns XP by difficulty. Amount habits earn partial XP for partial progress.
          • Streaks raise XP: +10% per full week of streak, up to +50%.
          • Levels get steep fast: each needs 30% more XP than the last through level 10, then 33% more through level 20. Level 20 takes about two years of near-perfect days.
          • Unfinished habits deal damage when the day ends. Partial progress reduces the damage. Each consecutive miss of the same habit hits harder: ×1, ×1.5, ×2, up to ×3.
          • HP regenerates 2 per day on its own. Each finished habit heals 1 more, and finishing every habit in a day heals 3 more.
          • At 0 HP you fail: you drop to the start of the previous level. The XP you lost stays as a bloodstain. Finish every habit the next day to recover it, or it is gone for good.
          • Each failure hollows you: max HP drops by 5, down to 25. Seven clean sweeps in a row restore it.
          • Each failure also lays a random curse for 3 days: Withering (−25% habit XP), Brittle (×1.5 damage), Marked (one habit deals double damage), Sapped (no HP regen) or Dulled (no focus XP). Curses stack.
          • Seven clean sweeps in a row also earn a bonfire (bank up to 2). Resting at one skips damage for the day, holds your streaks and refills HP.
          • Focus sessions earn half an XP per minute. Abandoning one costs 5 HP.
          • Rank comes from your average focus hours a day over the last 7 full days: the six ranks start at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 hours a day. Under 1 hour you are Ash. Each rank’s name stays hidden until you first reach it. The stages follow British Columbia’s 1–6 wildfire rank scale. It updates at midnight and drops as fast as it rises.
          • You can log or fix the last 7 days. Older days are locked.
