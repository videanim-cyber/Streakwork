    Achievements

      How scoring works
      • Finishing a habit earns XP by difficulty. Amount habits earn partial XP for partial progress.
      • Streaks raise XP: +10% per full week of streak, up to +50%.
      • Each level needs 20% more XP than the one before: 100, 120, 145, 175… Level 10 needs 515, level 20 needs about 3,200.
      • Unfinished habits deal damage when the day ends. Partial progress reduces the damage.
      • Each finished habit heals 1 HP. Finishing every habit in a day heals 3 more.
      • At 0 HP you are knocked out: progress toward the next level is lost and HP refills.
      • You can log or fix the last 7 days. Older days are locked.
